Zoodoodle support

Zoodoodle is an independent offline animal guide for family visits to Canberra’s National Zoo & Aquarium. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the zoo.

Explore together

Browse the animal guide, read or listen to short facts, follow the audio stories, and save favourites and sightings in your animal passport. There is no account or subscription.

Using the map

Map information is a snapshot of the zoo’s published information checked in November 2026. Routes are estimates and do not know about closures, steps or temporary works. Follow zoo signs and keeper advice. Some species have only a shared-area reference or no confirmed mapped area.

Listening offline

Facts, photographs, the map and two short species videos are bundled with the app. Narration uses Apple’s speech system. Available voices vary by device; download an English voice in iOS Accessibility settings before your visit if needed. Open Listening settings in the app to try the voice, change its pace or stop speech. Tour stories require manual playback and do not automatically advance.

Your passport

Sightings, favourites and listening choices are saved on your device. Zoodoodle does not provide an account or its own sync service. You can remove individual sightings and favourites in the app.

Get help

Email saphid@gmail.com with the app version, device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and steps that reproduce the problem.
