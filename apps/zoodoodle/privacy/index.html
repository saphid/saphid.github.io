Zoodoodle privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026. Developer: Alex Southwell.

Data collection

Zoodoodle does not collect personal information or send your sightings, favourites or listening choices to the developer. The app has no account, advertising, analytics or tracking service. There is no developer-operated app server.

Saved on your device

The app saves spotted animals, favourites, listening preferences, tour progress and colour choice on your device. Zoodoodle has no account sync or its own backup service. Your device settings determine whether iOS includes app data in its backups.

You can remove a sighting or favourite in the app. To remove all saved Zoodoodle data from this device, use Delete App in iOS Settings. Offload App keeps saved data. Manage any device backups separately in your Apple settings.

Speech and media

Animal facts, photographs, the map and videos are bundled with the app. Narration uses Apple’s speech synthesis and voices managed by iOS. Voice downloads depend on your device. Zoodoodle does not request microphone, speech-recognition or location access.

Links you choose to open

Source and licence links open external websites in your browser. Those sites may receive normal web-request information and follow their own privacy policies. Zoodoodle does not send your saved passport or listening history with these links.

Support

If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to send and uses it to answer your request. Contact saphid@gmail.com for support or privacy requests.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process normal website-request information under its Privacy Statement. The Zoodoodle app does not contact this website automatically.
