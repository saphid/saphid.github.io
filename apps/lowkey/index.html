Lowkey support

Lowkey is a compact, offline English keyboard for iPhone and iPad.

Turn on the keyboard

  1. Install Lowkey and open it.
  2. In Settings, go to General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard, then choose Lowkey.
  3. Tap a text field. Hold the globe key and select Lowkey.

Type and resize

Tap letters, or swipe across the letters of a word and lift. Tap an alternative above the keys to replace the last swiped word. Flick down for a key's small symbol, or up for uppercase. Tap 123 and then #+= for the symbol pages.

Hold Shift for settings, then choose Resize to drag the keyboard edge. You can also choose a preset. If a tiny height makes Shift hard to use, below 12 pt you can hold any key to open settings.

Full Access is optional

Typing and swipe recognition work offline without Full Access. The permission only lets the app and keyboard share preferences. If app settings do not appear in the keyboard, use its Shift menu, or optionally enable Allow Full Access in iOS keyboard settings.

Where Lowkey can be used

iOS switches to its own keyboard for password and some phone-number fields. Some apps disable custom keyboards. Lowkey's swipe word recognition is English and can make mistakes.

Get help

Email saphid@gmail.com with your Lowkey version, iOS version, device model and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not include passwords or private typing.
