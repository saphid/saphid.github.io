Lowkey privacy

Updated 19 November 2026.

Lowkey is an offline keyboard for iPhone and iPad by Alexander Southwell.

Your typing stays on your device

Lowkey processes key taps and swipe paths on your device to insert text into the app you are using. Lowkey does not save or transmit your typed text or swipe paths. Practice text in the Lowkey app stays in memory only.

Settings and local measurements

Lowkey saves your keyboard preferences on this device. Optional Allow Full Access lets the app and keyboard share preferences through an App Group. Typing and swipe recognition work without Full Access. Without that permission, use the keyboard's Shift menu to change its settings.

The optional keyboard height test saves local measurements of the screen and keyboard, without its text. Earlier research builds saved height-test editor text locally; version 1.0 build 16 removes that file on upgrade and no longer saves the editor text.

No data collection

Lowkey has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking, or network service. Its English word list is bundled with the app. The app does not collect data for its developer or send data to third parties. Data entered into another app is handled under that app's own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can type without Full Access, change preferences in the keyboard, remove Lowkey from the system's keyboard list, or uninstall the app. Removing the app removes its local app data. iOS controls system diagnostics and crash-report sharing separately.
