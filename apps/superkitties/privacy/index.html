SuperKitties Purr & Play privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026. Developer: Alexander Southwell.

The developer does not collect personal data through this game. The game has no accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics, purchases or chat. It does not connect to an app server.

Progress, settings, discoveries and play pictures are stored on your device. Play pictures stay inside the game; the game does not access your photo library. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Character voices are prepared audio files played on your device. The game does not record microphone audio or send speech to a server.

Removing the app removes its local app storage. Data retained in device backups is managed through Apple's backup settings.

If a grown-up emails support, the developer receives the information they choose to send and uses it to answer the request. Do not send children's personal information, passwords or private recordings. Contact saphid@gmail.com for support or privacy requests.

Support website

This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard request information, including IP addresses, when you visit it. See the GitHub privacy statement. The game itself does not load this website or send gameplay to it.
