SuperKitties Purr & Play support

Support information for the SuperKitties Purr & Play development app for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. This page does not announce a public App Store release.

Playing

Choose a kitty, then choose Purr & Play or a rescue adventure. On iPhone and iPad, drag the paw pad or tap a destination. Use the power button when practising powers. On Apple TV, use the Siri Remote directional controls or a paired controller, then Select/Play for a power and Menu to pause.

Settings and saved progress

Grown-up settings contains audio, voice accent, reduced motion and power practice options, plus an offline privacy policy. Progress, discoveries and play pictures are stored on the device. Removing the app removes local app storage; device backups are managed through Apple settings.

Get help

A grown-up can email saphid@gmail.com with the app version, device/OS and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not include children's personal information, credentials or private recordings.
