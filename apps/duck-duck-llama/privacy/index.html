Duck Duck Llama privacy policy

Effective 3 November 2026. Developer: Alexander Southwell.

Data collection

The developer does not collect personal data through this app. The app has no advertising, analytics or tracking, and no account or sign-in.

On your device

Your duck name, avatar choice, settings and achievements are stored only on your device. Games against bots run entirely on your device.

Online rooms

When you join an online room, the app sends your chosen duck name, a random reconnect code and your game moves to the game server. They are used only to run the live game and are shown to the other players in that room. The server log records connection numbers and room codes for troubleshooting. It does not record names, reconnect codes, cards or IP addresses. In this release, online rooms are limited to a private test server.

Storage and deletion

Deleting the app removes its stored data from your device. Room information on the game server ends when the room closes.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Support

If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to send and uses it only to answer your request. Contact saphid@gmail.com for support or privacy requests.
