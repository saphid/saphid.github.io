Duck Duck Llama privacy policy

Effective 3 November 2026. Developer: Alexander Southwell.

Summary

The app has no advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party data SDKs, and no account or sign-in. Games against bots run entirely on your device and send nothing to the developer.

On your device

Your duck name, avatar choice, settings, achievements and a random reconnect code are saved on your device.

Online rooms

Online rooms are limited to the developer's private test server in this release. When you join one, the app sends your duck name, avatar choice, reconnect code and game moves to that server.

  • What other players see: your duck name, avatar and the cards you play. They never see your reconnect code or the cards in your hand.
  • What the server keeps while it runs: the room you last joined, linked to your reconnect code, so you can rejoin after a dropped connection. This is held in memory and is cleared when the server restarts.
  • What the server log records: timestamps, room codes, connection numbers, seat numbers and public game events such as the cards played, their targets and the Count. The log does not record duck names, reconnect codes, hidden hands or the deck order. It is kept on the developer's own computer for troubleshooting and is not used for advertising.

Connections to the test server travel over Tailscale, the developer's private network provider. Tailscale may keep its own connection records under its own privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Data saved on your device stays until you delete the app, which removes it. The server's in-memory room and reconnect records are cleared when the server restarts. Server log entries have no fixed retention period yet: they are kept until the developer deletes them. Support emails stay in the developer's email account until deleted. To ask for server log entries or emails about you to be deleted, email the developer with the room code and approximate time, or the date of your email.

Your duck name

Your duck name is free text that other players in an online room can see. Please don't use your real name or other personal details as your duck name.

Support

If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to send and uses it only to answer your request. Contact saphid@gmail.com for support or privacy requests.
