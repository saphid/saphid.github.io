Duck Duck Llama privacy policy

Effective 3 November 2026. Developer: Alexander Southwell.

Summary

The app has no advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party data SDKs, and no account or sign-in. Games against bots run entirely on your device and send nothing to the developer.

On your device

Your duck name, avatar choice, settings, achievements and a random reconnect code are saved on your device.

Online rooms

Online rooms are limited to the developer's private test server in this release. When you join one, the app sends your duck name, avatar choice, reconnect code and game moves to that server.

  • What other players see: your duck name, avatar and the cards you play. They never see your reconnect code or the cards in your hand.
  • What the server keeps while it runs: the room you last joined, linked to your reconnect code, so you can rejoin after a dropped connection. This is held in memory and is cleared when the server restarts.
  • What the server log records: timestamps, room codes, connection numbers, seat numbers and public game events such as the cards played, their targets and the Count. The log does not record duck names, reconnect codes, hidden hands or the deck order. It is kept on the developer's own server for troubleshooting and is not shared or used for advertising.

The developer's private network provider and hosting may keep their own connection records.

Storage and deletion

Deleting the app removes the data saved on your device. To ask for server log entries about a game to be deleted, email the developer with the room code and approximate time.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Support

If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to send and uses it only to answer your request. Contact saphid@gmail.com for support or privacy requests.
