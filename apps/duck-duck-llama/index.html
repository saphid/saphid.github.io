Duck Duck Llama support

Duck Duck Llama is a card game for iPhone and iPad. Push the Count up and down, play Distract cards and call out the llama. To play on your own, tap “Play vs bots” on the title screen, then “Start the hunt!”. You can add or remove computer ducks in the lobby first, and the game works without an account or an internet connection.

How to play

Tap “How to play” on the title screen, or open How to Play from the menu during a game, for the rules and a guide to each card. With three to six players, a game has three rounds. A two-player duel continues until one player loses both duck cards. Turn on “Quick game” in the lobby for a single round (one duck card each in a duel).

Online rooms

Online rooms connect to a game server and are limited to a private test server in this release. If an online room does not connect, play against bots instead.

Settings

Settings include music and sound-effect volume, game tips, and a reduced-motion option that removes screen shake and other motion effects.

Get help

Email saphid@gmail.com with the app version, iOS version, device, and the steps that cause the problem. Do not send passwords or other personal information.
