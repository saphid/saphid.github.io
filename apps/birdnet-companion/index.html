BirdNET Companion support

BirdNET Companion is an independent iPhone and iPad app for people who run their own BirdNET-Go station. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the BirdNET or BirdNET-Go projects. Bird identification and recording happen on your station; the app does not use your device's microphone.

Try it without a server

On first launch, choose Offline Demo on the Connect screen to explore Live, Sightings, Alerts, Species and Settings with built-in sample data. No server, account or network is needed.

Connect your station

Enter your BirdNET-Go station address on the Connect screen. Public addresses need HTTPS; HTTP works only for local and private-network addresses. Make sure the station is running and that this device can reach it over your home network or your own VPN. iOS may ask for Local Network permission so the app can find a station on your network. Stations that require a password sign-in are not supported in this version.

Good to know

  • Alerts are local notifications created when the app syncs. iOS decides when background refresh runs, so alerts can be delayed; they are not real-time push notifications.
  • Saving settings, or locking, deleting or ignoring detections in the app, changes your real station.
  • A copy of recent station data is kept on your device for offline viewing.

Get help

Email saphid@gmail.com with the app version, iOS or iPadOS version, and steps that reproduce the problem. Do not send passwords, private station addresses or recordings.
