BirdNET Companion support

Choose “Offline Demo” in the station picker to explore without a server. For real detections, add your own BirdNET-Go station URL. Public hosts need HTTPS.

Connection help

Check that the server is running, its address is correct, and this device can reach it over your home network or VPN. Grant Local Network permission when connecting to a household server. Keep server credentials private.

Get help

Email saphid@gmail.com with the app version, iOS version, and steps that reproduce the problem. Do not send passwords, tokens, private footage, or personal media.
