BirdNET Companion privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026. Updated 3 November 2026 to describe the current app more precisely; data practices are unchanged. Developer: Alexander Southwell.

Data collection

The developer does not collect personal data through this app. The app has no accounts, advertising or tracking, and the App Store version sends no analytics or remote logs. There is no developer-operated server: the app connects only to the BirdNET-Go station you configure, and information processed by that station is governed by your own station setup.

What the app does on your network

The app requests detections, species information, images, audio clips and settings from the station address you enter. Live audio, when your station offers a stream, is played from your station and is not recorded by the app. The app never uses your device's microphone. To help you find a station, the app may check common local addresses such as birdnet-go.local on your local network; iOS asks for Local Network permission first, and you can decline it. Changes you choose to save in the app are sent to your station.

On your device

App settings and a cache of recent station data are stored on your device so you can review them when the station is unreachable. Observed species names are added to the on-device Spotlight index so you can search for them. Alerts are local notifications created on the device; this version does not register for remote push notifications. Removing the app removes its stored settings, cache and Spotlight entries. Data held on your BirdNET-Go station must be managed on that station.

Links and other services

If your station supplies a Wikipedia article link and you tap it, it opens in your browser and that site's policies apply. Your station may contact other services or keep logs; those are controlled by the station operator, not the developer.

Support

If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to send and uses it only to answer your request. Avoid including passwords, private station addresses or recordings. Contact saphid@gmail.com for support or privacy requests.
