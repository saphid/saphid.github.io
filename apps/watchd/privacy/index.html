Watchd Home privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026. Developer: Alexander Southwell.

Data collection

The developer does not collect personal data through this app. The app has no advertising or tracking. It connects directly to servers you choose; information processed by your server is governed by your server setup.

The app sends sign-in and playback requests to the media server address you enter. Session credentials use the device Keychain. Watch progress is stored locally for the demo and on your chosen server for live playback. No hosted library or developer account is required.

Storage and deletion

The app stores the last server address and demo watch progress on this iPhone. Signing out removes the saved session token for the connected server but keeps its address; leaving the demo keeps demo progress. The app has no video download or export feature. Removing the app clears its sandbox settings and demo progress. Keychain items can remain outside the sandbox; sign out before removing the app to remove the current server's saved token. Account information, watch history, media and backups held by a household server are controlled by that server's operator.

In version 1.0.0 build 3 and later, Settings > Account > Delete my account can request permanent account deletion from a compatible updated Watchd Home server. The flow requires your password or configured PIN and explicit confirmation. The matching server removes your account, watch history and all sign-in sessions; shared media files, other accounts and operator-managed backups remain. Older servers must be updated before this action can succeed. Deleting the final owner account removes administration access and does not reopen public setup. The demo creates no account. Build 2 does not include this deletion flow.

Network services

Your configured server may contact other services or keep access logs. Those services are controlled by the server operator. The developer does not receive your server credentials or your library through an app backend.

Support

If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to send and uses it to answer your request. Avoid including credentials or private media. Contact saphid@gmail.com for support or privacy requests.
