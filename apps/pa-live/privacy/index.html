PA Live privacy policy

Effective 3 November 2026. Developer: Alexander Southwell.

Data collection

The developer does not run an account service or backend for PA Live and does not receive your messages, calls, bots or jobs. The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Your own server

PA Live connects to the server you pair it with. Your messages, call transcripts, bots, notes, jobs, approvals and feedback are stored on that server, which you control. Delete them in the app or on your server. Pairing credentials are stored on your device.

Voice calls

When you start a voice call, your device sends call audio directly to the AI voice service your server is set up to use (currently OpenAI). Your server sets up the call using your own account with that service. That service handles the audio under its own terms and privacy policy, which apply to your account. When a bot replies to typed messages, your server sends those messages to the same service.

Microphone, camera and local network

The microphone is used only during voice calls and dictation. Dictation is transcribed on your device. The camera is used only to scan a pairing code; images are not stored or sent. Local network access is used to reach your server.

Optional T3 Connect sign-in

The app's Workspace area includes an optional T3 Connect sign-in from the open-source T3 Code project, used to reach servers through the T3 Connect relay. It is not required for PA Live. If you sign in, your email address and account identifier are processed by T3 Connect and its sign-in provider under their terms. The developer of PA Live does not operate that service or receive that information.

Storage and deletion

App settings and cached information remain on your device. Removing a connection removes its saved configuration. Removing the app clears its sandbox storage. Data on your server must be managed on that server.

Children

PA Live is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Support

If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to send and uses it to answer your request. Avoid including credentials, pairing links or private conversations. Contact saphid@gmail.com for support or privacy requests.
