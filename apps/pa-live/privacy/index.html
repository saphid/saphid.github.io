PA Live privacy policy

Effective 4 November 2026. Developer: Alexander Southwell.

Data collection

The developer does not run an account service or backend for PA Live and does not receive your messages, calls, bots or jobs. The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Your own server

PA Live connects to the server you pair it with. Your messages, call transcripts, bots, notes, jobs, approvals and feedback are stored on that server, which you control. In the app you can delete bots and individual scratchpad entries. Deleting a bot keeps its past jobs and their threads, and does not erase its conversation history. Other stored records are managed by whoever operates the server. Pairing credentials are stored on your device.

AI services

PA Live's bots answer typed messages and voice calls using the AI service your server is set up to use for chat and voice (currently OpenAI), under your own account with that service. To answer a typed message, your server sends the message and the context the bot needs, such as recent conversation, the bot's instructions, its scratchpad notes, job details and tool results, to that service. When you start a voice call, your server sets up the call with similar context, and your device then sends call audio directly to that service.

Background jobs run as threads on your server, or on another server you have connected, using the AI worker chosen for the bot or job. Depending on your setup this may be a different provider, for example Anthropic for Claude workers or OpenAI for Codex workers. A job sends its task and the context of its work to that provider, and its results can return to the bot's AI service.

Each service processes and may retain this information under its own terms and privacy policy, which apply to your account: OpenAI privacy policy, Anthropic privacy policy. Your server's configuration determines which services are used. Manage or delete information held by a service through your account with it.

Installed tools

If you install tool servers (MCP servers) for a bot, your server runs them or connects to the address you give, and sends them the requests the bot makes with the details those requests need. What they do with that information depends on who operates them. Settings you enter for a tool server, such as keys, are kept on your server. Removing a server from a bot's Tools tab stops the bot from using it.

Microphone, camera and local network

The microphone is used only during voice calls and dictation. Dictation is transcribed on your device. The camera is used only to scan a pairing code; images are not stored or sent. Local network access is used to reach your server.

Optional T3 Connect sign-in

The app's Workspace area includes an optional T3 Connect sign-in from the open-source T3 Code project, used to reach servers through the T3 Connect relay. It is not required for PA Live. If you sign in, your email address and account identifier are processed by T3 Connect and its sign-in provider, Clerk, under their terms: Clerk privacy policy. Manage that account from its account settings in the app, or contact T3 Connect about it. The developer of PA Live does not operate that service or receive that information.

Storage and deletion

App settings, pairing credentials and cached information remain on your device. Removing a connection removes its saved configuration, and removing the app clears its sandbox storage. Removing the app does not delete information on your server or held by the AI service or T3 Connect. In the app you can delete bots and scratchpad entries; other records on your server, such as conversation history and jobs, are managed by the server operator. Manage information held by those services through your accounts with them.

Children

PA Live is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Support

If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to send and uses it to answer your request. Avoid including credentials, pairing links or private conversations. Contact saphid@gmail.com for support or privacy requests.
