StoryShelf privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026. Updated 3 November 2026. Developer: Alexander Southwell.

Data collection

The developer does not collect personal data through this app. The app has no advertising or tracking. It connects directly to servers you choose; information processed by your server is governed by your server setup.

Listener profiles, imported books and playback progress are stored on your device; the app does not sync them to a server. Books you play from a household server come from that server. Read-along text for imported spoken audio can be generated with on-device speech recognition after you allow Speech Recognition, where the device supports it. The app does not record from the microphone. Local Network access supports server discovery: the app uses Bonjour to find nearby servers and checks their manifests before showing suggestions. A parent chooses which libraries to add in Parents. Remote playback control is disabled in this release.

Storage and deletion

App settings and cached information remain on your device. Removing a connection removes its saved configuration; cached or downloaded content may remain. Removing the app clears its sandbox storage. Exported files, Keychain credentials and information shared with system services may remain outside that storage and require separate cleanup. Data on a household server must be managed on that server.

Network services

Your configured server may contact other services or keep access logs. An added server can supply audio and artwork addresses on other hosts, which the app may contact directly; those hosts may receive network information such as your IP address and may keep request logs. Use servers you trust. Those services are controlled by the server operator. The developer does not receive your server credentials or your library through an app backend.

Support

If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to send and uses it to answer your request. Avoid including credentials or private media. Contact saphid@gmail.com for support or privacy requests.
