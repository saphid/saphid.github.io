StoryShelf privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026. Developer: Alexander Southwell.

Data collection

The developer does not collect personal data through this app. The app has no advertising or tracking. It connects directly to servers you choose; information processed by your server is governed by your server setup.

Listener profiles, imported books and playback progress stay on your device and your household server. Optional read-along generation uses on-device speech recognition for imported audio. The app does not record from the microphone. Local Network access supports server discovery. Discovered libraries are suggestions; an adult must approve a source in Parents. Remote playback control is disabled in this release.

Storage and deletion

App settings and cached information remain on your device. You can remove a configured connection in the app or remove the app to clear its local app data. Credentials may remain in the device Keychain. Data on a household server must be managed on that server.

Network services

Your configured server may contact other services or keep access logs. Those services are controlled by the server operator. The developer does not receive your server credentials or your library through an app backend.

Support

If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to send and uses it to answer your request. Avoid including credentials or private media. Contact saphid@gmail.com for support or privacy requests.
