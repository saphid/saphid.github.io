Saphid Duo Viewer

A native iPhone viewer for Twitch, with live video beside native chat and adjustable layouts.

Connect your Twitch account to browse live channels, followed streams and categories, search for channels, and join chat. Choose video with chat, slim chat, or full video. Place chat beside or below video and adjust the split.

A Twitch account and internet connection are required for discovery and chat. Some streams require a separate sign-in inside Twitch’s official player. Subscriptions, Bits, channel points and creator tools are handled on Twitch. Picture in Picture is not advertised as a supported feature.

Support and safety

Email saphid@gmail.com for help, a privacy request, or a report of abusive chat. Include the channel, message ID or description, and approximate time when available. Do not include passwords or access tokens.

In the app, long-press a chat message to report it by email or block its author locally. Blocking removes their displayed messages and hides future messages in this app. Account → Blocked chat users lets you unblock them. This does not change your Twitch account’s block list. Twitch and channel moderators also moderate chat; you can report violations directly to Twitch.
