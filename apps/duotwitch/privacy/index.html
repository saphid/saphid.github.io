Privacy policy

Foldstream (bundle com.saphid.duotwitch) is an independent Twitch viewer developed by Saphid. This policy covers the App Store candidate. Updated 9 November 2026.

Your Twitch account and content

Sign-in takes place on Twitch’s approval page through the system authentication session. The app receives an access token, refresh token, account ID, login name and granted permissions. Tokens are stored in this app’s device Keychain without iCloud synchronization. We do not receive your Twitch password. Twitch’s approval page still identifies the existing connection as Saphid Duo Viewer; the registered public client and saved connection are unchanged.

The app sends your authorization token and API requests directly to Twitch for browsing, followed channels and chat. Chat you choose to send goes to Twitch and is visible according to the channel’s settings. Received chat is kept in a bounded in-memory timeline (up to 500 messages); drafts are kept in memory. These are not sent to the developer’s diagnostic receiver.

Blocked chat user IDs and login names are stored in app preferences to hide messages across channels and relaunches. Unblocking removes the saved entry. Signing out clears this local block list, the chat timeline and drafts, and removes the app’s saved authorization credentials. Uninstalling an iOS app may leave Keychain entries; sign out first if you want to remove the saved sign-in.

Twitch’s player and other services

Video plays through Twitch’s official web player on an HTTPS page hosted at saphid.app using GitHub Pages. Twitch’s player can use its own cookies, storage, advertising and separate account sign-in. Signing out of the native account does not sign out of Twitch’s player or your system browser. Twitch, its content delivery services and the page host receive network information such as your IP address when providing their services. Their retention is governed by their own policies.

See Twitch’s privacy notice and GitHub’s privacy statement. The native app contains no advertising or analytics SDK and does not use an advertising identifier. Twitch’s embedded service may show ads and process information under its own notice.

Twitch’s published notices describe retained user and device/browser identifiers, IP-derived general location, viewing and search activity, and advertising interactions. Twitch and its advertising partners may use identifiers and interactions to personalize and measure advertising across services. Our App Store privacy disclosures include these partner practices conservatively; they do not describe all playback as untracked. The native app does not collect precise location or access your advertising identifier.

This candidate sends the browser’s Global Privacy Control signal to the player and its resources. Twitch controls its response to that signal; it is not a guarantee that all cross-company advertising use stops. This build does not present Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission prompt or implement an independently verified Twitch consent interface. See Twitch’s cookie notice and privacy choices for its published controls. Diagnostic consent below is separate from Twitch’s data practices.

Playback diagnostics

Diagnostic recording and automatic uploads are off by default. Account → Playback diagnostics → Record and share diagnostics lets you opt in to rolling playback logs on your device and automatic sending of a filtered subset to the developer’s private HTTPS receiver at lxso1.tail4e5636.ts.net over Tailscale. A connection to that private network is needed for upload; it is not needed for ordinary viewing. If the receiver cannot be reached, uploads retry within a bounded queue and expire after 24 hours. Turning the control off stops new recording, cancels uploads and clears unsent queued records. Previously saved local logs remain available for you to share; data already received expires under the retention limit below. Viewing works without enabling diagnostics.

Diagnostics include app/build and OS versions, event timestamps, video timing and performance, player state, layout dimensions, audio event types and random identifiers for one app run or player session. Automatic uploads exclude device uptime, tokens, passwords, account and channel identity, chat text, media URLs and full error descriptions. The receiver does not deliberately record request IP addresses. Diagnostics are not linked to your Twitch account and are used to diagnose playback problems, not for advertising or tracking.

Local rolling logs use at most four files of 256 KiB each, excluded from device backup, and overwrite older records as they fill. Current logs measure elapsed time within the app run. Older beta logs may contain device uptime; old queued records containing it are rejected by the new upload boundary. The upload queue holds at most 512 records and 512 KiB of record payload. The receiver expires records after 24 hours and caps stored payload at 10,000 records or 4 MiB, evicting older batches when needed. Uploads can be incomplete because these limits can evict records.

Account → Playback diagnostics → Share playback logs lets you choose a destination for a local copy. Review that copy before sharing; the recipient then controls its retention. Removing the app removes its local diagnostic files.

Support, reports and privacy requests

A safety report opens an email draft only when you choose it. The draft includes the selected message’s text, author login and message ID; nothing is sent until you send the email. Support emails, including their contents and your address, are processed by the developer’s email provider and retained as needed to handle the request. You may request deletion by contacting saphid@gmail.com. Avoid sending passwords or tokens.

There is no separate Saphid account to create or delete. To disconnect, sign out in Account; you can also revoke the app’s access in Twitch’s Connections settings. Twitch account deletion and Twitch-hosted messages are managed by Twitch. For developer-held data deletion or questions, contact the email above. The app is not intended for children under Twitch’s minimum age or applicable local age requirements.
